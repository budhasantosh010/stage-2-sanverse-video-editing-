# Panel Responsive Contract

- Desktop: nested resizable panels are visible and bounded by contract minima/maxima.
- Tablet: AI becomes an overlay; media and tool regions may collapse while preview and timeline remain usable.
- Compact: video stays first, AI remains an accessible overlay, and side regions do not force page-level horizontal overflow.
- Resize completion persists layout; transient pointer movement does not write storage.
- Separators support pointer and keyboard resizing with an enlarged hit target and visible focus.
- Reduced-motion preference disables nonessential transitions.
