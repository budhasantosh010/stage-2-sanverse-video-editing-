# Evidence — cutting, with edits anchored to the footage (G5-B)

Date: 2026-07-28
Evidence level: **E4** — real media, real cut, real export, exported frames
inspected. Not E5: one recording, one frame rate, no owner run.

## What was verified by running it

Project `project_c6121ed23762e2fc188c0d18e706b11d`, the owner's own 14.605-second
1920x1080 30 fps recording, opened in the browser at `localhost:2000`.

### 1. The saved file upgraded itself, losslessly

On open, the file moved from `sanverse.project/v2` to `sanverse.project/v3`
and was rewritten once.

```
before   schemaVersion sanverse.project/v2   revision 11
after    schemaVersion sanverse.project/v3   revision 11
```

- Clips gained `gainDb`, `fadeIn`, `fadeOut`, all neutral.
- All three nameplates converted from `clipId` + `compositionInterval` to
  `assetId` + `sourceInterval`. None blocked.
- Provenance preserved exactly: `direct`, `ai`, `ai`.
- Revision did not move, because an upgrade is not an edit.
- Zero browser console errors. Zero server errors.

### 2. Cutting works from the screen

| Action | Result |
|---|---|
| Playhead to 4 s, "Cut here" | Two sections, 27.3879% / 72.6121% — 4 / 14.605 exactly |
| History entry | "Cut at 4.0s" |
| Playhead to 2 s, "Remove this section" | One section, gap closed |
| History entry | "Removed a section and closed the gap" |
| "Cut here" at 0 s | Refused: "This is already the edge of a section." Nothing sent |
| "Remove this section" with one section left | Refused: "This is the only section left." Nothing sent |

### 3. Edits followed the footage, or were blocked — never moved silently

Before the cut, the three nameplates were anchored to recording-seconds
`0→5`, `0→4`, and `0→3`. Recording-seconds `0→4` were then deleted.

```
  survivor   "Sanverse Video editing"   recording 0→5
             only 4→5 survived, so it now shows at 0→1 on screen
             it moved with the footage; nobody told it to

  blocked    "Santosh"                  recording 0→4   SOURCE_SPAN_REMOVED
  blocked    "Priya"                    recording 0→3   SOURCE_SPAN_REMOVED
             their footage is gone, so they draw nothing and the
             history says "needs attention" beside each of them
```

### 4. The exported MP4 agrees with the screen

```
duration     10.605000 s      (14.605 - the 4 s removed, exactly)
video        h264 1920x1080 30/1
audio        aac 48000 Hz 2 channels
```

Frames pulled straight out of the exported file:

- **0.5 s** — "Sanverse Video editing / Edits for Busineses" is drawn.
- **3.0 s** — no nameplate, because it ended at 1.0 s.
- Neither blocked nameplate appears anywhere.

### 5. `-c:a copy` is gone

The exported audio is `aac 48000 Hz stereo`, produced by the filter graph, not
copied. Copied audio can only be cut at its own compression block boundaries and
drifted out of sync at the first cut. This was the recorded G5-B blocker and it
is closed.

### 6. Undo took the cuts back

Two undos returned the project to one 14.605-second section with all three
nameplates unblocked at `0→5`, `0→4`, `0→3`. The owner's project was left
exactly as it was found.

## A real defect the browser found, that the tests did not

Removing the opening section leaves the `<video>` element parked at a moment the
finished video no longer contains. `advancePlayback` treated "not inside the
expected stretch" as "moved past it", and reported the video as **ended** —
freezing the preview on a stale frame right after a cut.

Fixed by distinguishing three cases: still inside the expected stretch; inside
some other stretch (the user dragged the browser's own scrubber); or before the
expected stretch, which means the footage changed under a playhead that did not
move, and the answer is to show the new beginning. Two regression tests added.

This is the third time the real browser has found something the suite could not.

## Limitations — what this does NOT prove

- **One recording, one frame rate.** Only 30/1 constant frame rate on one file.
  Variable frame rate, 30000/1001, and rational-rate fixtures are G5B-13 and
  have not been run.
- **Trim, reorder, and clip audio have no control on screen.** They are built,
  tested, and reach the export, but the owner cannot get to them yet.
- **Holes are never created from the screen.** The button always closes the gap.
  Black-and-silence holes are implemented in the domain, the preview, and the
  export, and are covered by tests, but no real hole has been exported.
- **The owner has not run this.** G5B-01 and G5B-14 are owner gates and remain
  open.
- **Mixed change sets are a known rough edge.** A change set holding both a cut
  and an overlay can have the cut applied while being reported blocked for its
  overlay. No such change set exists today; ADR-005 records that G7 must resolve
  it before compound requests ship.
