# Nested panel constraints

- AI: 320–420 px expanded; 52 px collapsed.
- Upper workspace: minimum 300 px on bounded desktop/tablet.
- Timeline: minimum 240 px; maximum 65%.
- Media: 220–420 px when present.
- Preview: minimum 480 px desktop; 280 px compact.
- Tool: 300–520 px when present.

At 1024 px, AI becomes an off-canvas overlay and Media/Tool collapse, leaving Preview 940×309 and Timeline 940×258. Below 981 px, Preview and Timeline leave the flex-percentage authority and derive natural height from content.
